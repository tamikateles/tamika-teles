Consumer Research · Undergraduate Thesis 2025–2026

AI-Generated Luxury Cues & Consumer Perception

How do luxury-focused visual cues influence consumer spending decisions, and does AI disclosure change their effectiveness? This thesis examines how exposure to AI-generated luxury and nature cues affects impulsive spending behavior. Using an experimental research design, the project investigates the intersection of consumer psychology, emerging technology, and decision-making, providing insights into how brands can use visual communication to influence consumer behavior in an increasingly AI-driven marketplace.

Sustainability & Consumer Behavior

Why Greenwashing Is a Consumer Trust Problem, Not a PR Problem

Most brands treat greenwashing as a communications risk, something to manage when it gets noticed. But the deeper problem is what it does to consumer trust over time. When people stop believing sustainability claims mean anything, the entire market for responsible business shrinks.
